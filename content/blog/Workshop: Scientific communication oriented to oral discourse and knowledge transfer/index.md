**Xartec Salut**, **IRIS** and the **Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC)** invite you to a hands-on training day dedicated to **scientific communication oriented to oral discourse and knowledge transfer**.

- **Date & time:** 19 November 2025, 16:00–20:00
- **Venue:** Casa Bonay – NICA Room, Barcelona (Spain)
- **Format:** Free in-person workshop with networking break

## Agenda highlights

1. Welcome remarks by **Anna Castilló** (Promotora de Recerca i Innovació, Xartec Salut).
2. Lessons learned and good practices from a **Xartec Salut member**.
3. Two-part **training workshop** led by **Salva Farré (Eduscopi)** with a networking coffee break in between.

{{< figure src="agenda.svg" title="Program schedule for the Share & Connect workshop" >}}

## Why join

- Gain practical tips to strengthen your oral presentations and public engagement.
- Learn strategies to transfer research outcomes to industry, institutions, and the public.
- Expand your network with peers and facilitators in the Catalonia research ecosystem.

Stay tuned for registration details and feel free to share the announcement with colleagues who could benefit from refining their scientific storytelling skills.
